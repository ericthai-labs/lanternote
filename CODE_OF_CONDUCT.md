# Code of Conduct

Lanternote is a small project, and we want it to be a friendly place to ask, report and build.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new or who write in a second language.
- Assume good intent; ask before you judge.
- Keep feedback about the work, specific and useful.
- Respect people's time: search before you ask, and say when your question is answered.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Personal attacks, or pressure on people to reply or to do work for free.
- Sharing someone's private information without their permission.
- Spam and self-promotion unrelated to Lanternote.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, lock threads, and block people who do not follow this code. To report a problem privately, open a [security advisory](https://github.com/ericthai-labs/lanternote/security/advisories/new) marked "conduct" — it is visible only to the maintainers.

This applies to the repository, its issues, pull requests and discussions.
